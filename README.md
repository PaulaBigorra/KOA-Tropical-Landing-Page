#  KOA Tropical | Landing Page

> A tropical boutique hotel landing page focused on natural elegance,
> ocean serenity, and a memorable guest experience.

🚧 **Status: In Development**

---

##  About the Project

**KOA Tropical** is a landing page concept for a fictional tropical
boutique hotel and resort.

The project aims to create an immersive digital experience that
communicates the atmosphere of the hotel before the guest even arrives.

The visual identity combines tropical landscapes, natural materials,
warm neutral tones, ocean views, and elegant typography to create a
sense of relaxation, comfort, and connection with nature.

The project is currently under development, with the interface being
designed in Figma and progressively implemented as a web application.

The long-term goal is to develop the website as a **Progressive Web App
(PWA)**, allowing the experience to potentially be adapted into a
mobile application in the future.

---

##  The Challenge

Many hotel websites focus heavily on presenting information without
creating a strong emotional connection with the destination.

The challenge behind KOA Tropical is to create a digital experience
that allows visitors to understand the atmosphere, identity, and
services of the hotel before they arrive.

The project focuses on making important information easy to discover
while maintaining a visually immersive experience.

KOA Tropical aims to combine:

- A strong and consistent visual identity
- Intuitive navigation
- Immersive imagery
- Clear room information
- Event and celebration options
- Location and nearby experiences
- Guest reviews
- Contact and reservation information
- A responsive experience across different devices

The goal is to make the website feel like the first step of the
guest's experience with the hotel.

##  Design & Visual Identity

The visual identity of KOA Tropical is inspired by the relationship
between tropical nature, architecture, and the ocean.

The design language combines:

- 🌿 Tropical nature
- 🌊 Ocean and coastal landscapes
- 🪵 Natural wood
- 🏖️ Sand and beige tones
- 🌱 Natural and iguana green tones
- ☀️ Warm lighting
- 🌴 Tropical vegetation
- 🛏️ Comfortable and elegant interiors

The objective is to create a visual experience that feels:

**Natural · Elegant · Relaxing · Warm · Tropical**

The design was initially developed in **Figma** before being translated
into the web interface.

##  Progressive Web App

One of the main goals of KOA Tropical is to develop the website with
a **PWA-first approach**.

A Progressive Web App can provide a more app-like experience while
still being accessible through the web.

The planned PWA functionality may include:

- Installable application
- Responsive mobile experience
- App-like navigation
- Offline capabilities
- Service worker
- Web app manifest
- Mobile-friendly interface

The PWA approach also creates a foundation that could later be used
to develop a dedicated mobile application.
